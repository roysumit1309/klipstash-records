Privacy Policy

KlipStash Records — last updated 21 September 2026

KlipStash Records has no servers. There is no account to create, no database we operate, and no endpoint that receives your documents. This policy is short because the honest answer to most privacy questions here is "that never leaves your own devices and your own Google Drive".

What the capture page does

The capture page runs entirely in your browser. When you photograph a receipt it is re-encoded on your phone, stored in your browser's local database, and uploaded directly from your browser to your own Google Drive. It is never routed through any machine we control.

What we ask Google for, and what we cannot see

We request a single Google Drive permission, drive.file. Google describes it as non-sensitive, and it is the narrowest scope that can do this job. It grants access only to files this application itself creates.

That is a limit enforced by Google, not a promise by us: this application cannot read the rest of your Drive — not your documents, not your photos, not files other applications put there — even if it tried.

What is stored, and where

  • Photos you take — held in your browser's local storage only until they reach your Drive, then deleted from the phone. They are never copied anywhere else.
  • Your Google sign-in — the capture page holds a short-lived access token in memory for about an hour. It is never written to disk. The desktop application stores a renewal token in Windows Credential Manager, on your machine.
  • Two non-secret values — your Google client identifier and the identifier of the Drive folder, kept in your browser so you do not have to enter them again.

What the desktop application does

Text recognition runs entirely on your own computer, using the recognizer built into Windows. No image and no recognised text is ever uploaded for processing. Your records are stored in a database on your machine.

The application contacts Google Drive only, and only to sign in and to read the folder it created.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no cookies. We do not know who uses this, how often, or what they photograph. We have no way to find out.

Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, because we do not have your data. The only third party involved is Google, whose handling of your Drive is governed by Google's own privacy policy and your agreement with them.

KlipStash Records' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke this application's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Your files stay in your Drive; only our ability to see the folder goes away. Deleting the folder in Drive deletes the uploaded copies. Records already on your computer are removed by deleting them in the application.

Children

This application is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because nothing is collected, there is no history of yours for a future version to start treating differently.
